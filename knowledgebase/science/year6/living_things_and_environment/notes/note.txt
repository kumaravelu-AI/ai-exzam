Living Things and Environment (Year 6)

Food Chain:
A food chain shows how food moves from one living thing to another.
Plants are producers. Animals are consumers.

Competition:
Living things compete for food, water, space and mates.

Relationships:
Parasitism means one organism benefits and the other is harmed.

Adaptation:
Plants have special features like small leaves and deep roots to survive.

Investigation:
Manipulated variable is what we change.
Responding variable is what we observe.
