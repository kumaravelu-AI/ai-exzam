Microorganisms are tiny living things. They are too small to be seen clearly with the naked eye.
Examples of microorganisms include bacteria, fungi, protozoa, and some algae.

Some microorganisms are useful.
For example, bacteria can help make yogurt and bread production can involve yeast.

Some microorganisms are harmful.
They can spoil food or cause disease.

Students should understand:
1. microorganisms are tiny living things
2. not all microorganisms are harmful
3. some microorganisms are useful in daily life
